## Result and Boundaries

Dates returned by our picker now retain whether the field meant a whole day or a timed instant. `Form.DatePicker.Type.Date` means a day even when the preserved Date contains a noon/nonmidnight timestamp. `Type.DateTime` and the existing default mean a timed instant, including midnight. `isFullDay(null)` and `isFullDay(undefined)` return false. The existing `FormDatePicker` alias shares the same helper.

Only fresh Dates produced by the owned decode/clone paths get private WeakMap intent and the original native timestamp. Change, focus, blur, reset and submit use the known picker mode when they materialize those values. No public flag, extra Date property, prototype/global monkeypatch, clock heuristic or wire/cache bit was added. The Form keeps current field modes alongside its own values; changing a picker mode without changing its timestamp also updates registration before subsequent submission.

Plain external Dates, external clones, invalid Dates, forged public `fullDay` flags and brandless Date-like objects remain explicitly unsupported. A previously known Date with a changed/invalid native timestamp also rejects. This checks the current timestamp against the original; it does not claim mutation-history interception, private-bit parity or protection from arbitrary trusted Host-code monkeypatching. Already-returned Dates retain their original materialized intent, not a later field setting. The WeakMap does not retain the returned Date or Form by itself.

The existing canonical ISO/null renderer data, incoming validation, date limits, accepted-submit rules, clone isolation and string Cache tuples remain unchanged. A cold `DateTime` value at midnight stays an instant; reads do not migrate old data or infer day intent. The new mode-switch fixture asserts the original raw Cache bytes remain identical across successful submissions, caller mutation of Dates and a cold open. There is no renderer, manager, child, protocol, storage, permission or native-effect code change.
